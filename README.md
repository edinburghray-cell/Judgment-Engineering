Judgment Engineering
Research on preserving human reasoning as organizational memory for AI-assisted decision making.
Introduction
Organizations preserve decisions through documents, reports, and databases. However, the reasoning behind those decisions often disappears.
Judgment Engineering explores how AI systems can capture, preserve, and reuse the judgment behind important decisions so future teams inherit not only what happened, but why decisions were made.

Research Question
How can organizations capture, preserve, and improve the reasoning behind important decisions instead of preserving only the outcomes?
Vision
To create a framework where organizational judgment becomes a preserved, searchable, and reusable asset through human-AI collaboration.
Core Principles
Judgment Engineering is based on four principles:
Decisions should preserve reasoning, not only outcomes.
Organizational judgment is a reusable asset.
AI can structure human reasoning into searchable organizational memory.
Better preserved judgment leads to better future decisions.
Research Framework
